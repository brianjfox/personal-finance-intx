# Corbits Personal Finance 0.3.1

Everything in 0.3.0, plus the cash the Market Manager was not seeing.

## What changed

- **Cash on hand counts your bank balances.** The drift report used to
  count cash only from a brokerage "cash" line, so a household whose
  cash sits in checking and savings accounts — or as an exchange's USD
  balance — showed "cash $0.00", the Market Manager reasoned from "no
  cash to pay tax from", and the Auditor's tax-cash check ran against
  nothing. Open cash accounts and the cash balances of investment
  accounts now count, in the plan's currency. A balance in another
  currency is not summed at face value or silently dropped: the page
  says "(+ €62,565.41 not converted)" next to the figure.

## From 0.3.0 (unpublished)

- **Coinbase tax lots** derived from the account's complete transaction
  history (FIFO; transfers in are honest "basis unknown" lots; Coinbase
  Pro's own trades are not available through the API).
- **The Auditor tells caveats from blocks**: unknown-basis lots reach
  your queue with a "Before you sign" note; short-term gains on known
  lots still block unless the Market Manager acknowledges them on the
  record. Verdicts store caveats (a one-time ledger migration runs on
  first start).
- **Proposals never exceed the position** (no more "sell 99 BTC of 83").
- **Closed and hidden accounts are no longer holdings** to the drift
  engine, the agents' tools, or the Auditor.
- Housekeeping: unchanged tax lots write no nightly facts; the
  no-proposal banner lost its stray second period.

## After upgrading

Run an update on Coinbase (or wait for tonight's) to populate the lots;
the next proposal will use them and will see your cash.
