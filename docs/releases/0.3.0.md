# Corbits Personal Finance 0.3.0

The Market Manager can finally get a crypto trade in front of you: real
tax lots from Coinbase, an Auditor that distinguishes "unknown" from
"bad", proposals sized to what you actually hold, and holdings that
exclude the accounts you closed.

## What changed

- **Coinbase tax lots.** Coinbase reports no lots, but its transaction
  history is complete, so every update now walks it and builds FIFO
  lots: purchases and fills carry their real basis (including the
  commission), staking and earn payouts their value on receipt, and
  sales consume the oldest lots first. Coins that *arrived* by transfer
  — including everything migrated from Coinbase Pro in November 2023 —
  become honest "basis unknown" lots, because Coinbase only knows what
  they were worth on arrival, not what you paid. Lots are kept only when
  the history adds up to the balance exactly; otherwise they are
  withheld and the fetch log says why. (Coinbase Pro's own trade history
  is not available through the API.)
- **The Auditor tells caveats from blocks.** A sale drawing on lots with
  an unknown basis is no longer refused — it reaches your queue cleared,
  with a "Before you sign" note on the card saying the holding period
  and basis could not be verified. A sale that would realize a
  *short-term* gain on known lots is still blocked — unless the Market
  Manager acknowledges the short-term treatment on the record, in which
  case it clears with that acknowledgement shown to you. Verdicts now
  store these caveats (a one-time ledger migration runs on first start).
- **Proposals never exceed the position.** A sell candidate is capped at
  the whole units the chosen position holds; the earlier "sell 99 BTC of
  83" cannot recur. The rationale says when a cap applied.
- **Closed and hidden accounts are no longer holdings.** The drift
  report, the Market Manager's positions tool, the Strategist's and
  estate planner's account lists, and the Auditor's checks now see only
  open accounts — the same view the dashboard already had. Removing an
  institution or hiding an account really removes it from the agents'
  picture; history is untouched.
- **Housekeeping.** An unchanged tax lot no longer writes a new ledger
  fact every night; the no-proposal banner lost its stray second period.

## After upgrading

Run an update on Coinbase (or wait for tonight's) to populate the lots;
the Strategy page's next proposal will use them.
