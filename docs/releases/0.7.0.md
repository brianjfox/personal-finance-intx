# Corbits Personal Finance 0.7.0

**Updates install themselves from now on.** Check for Updates… in the
application menu fetches the newest published release, compares it with
the one you are running, and on Update downloads the installer, checks
its signature against the key built into the app, installs it over the
current bundle, and relaunches. This is the first release that carries
the updater, so 0.7.0 itself installs by hand once; every release after
it arrives through the menu.

**Crypto is in your cash flow.** Coinbase, Kraken, and watch-only
wallets now report transactions over the same rolling window the bank
connectors use, so buys, sells, swaps, transfers, and staking income
show in the Flow Summary and are visible to the Ledger Analyst. Kraken
and on-chain legs are valued at the day's spot price, and the
description says so. A coin sent between two of your own accounts is
one internal transfer, not an outflow and an inflow: the two sides
value the leg differently and the fee comes off the received quantity,
so they now pair on quantity within a small tolerance rather than on an
equal amount.

**The nightly wakes the Market Manager.** A plan that opts in starts a
proposal after any clean nightly that finds a class outside its band,
the same proposal the Propose button starts. It never fires while a
decision awaits you or a proposal is running, never twice for the same
drift picture inside a week, and never after a one-institution refresh.
Every wake and every skip is a journal line, and the Strategy page
shows what last night did. Existing plans are off until you tick the
new box in the plan editor; a new plan starts with it on.

**The Market Manager no longer chokes on a large position.** Its reply
is now its choice of candidate, thesis and confidence; the draft is
rebuilt from that choice by the same deterministic engine, so a position
with a thousand tax lots no longer produces a draft the model cannot
repeat. For planning, an order's same-day fills fold into one
conceptual lot, and the approval card lists "first fill +237 fills" on
a day instead of 238 lot ids.

**Tax lots read against their day.** The lot list under a position's
cost basis folds an order's same-day fills into one row, so a Coinbase
position bought in hundreds of fills is a list you can scan. Every row
shows what the lot cost per unit, the day's average with its low-to-
high range from Coinbase Exchange's daily candle for the asset (one
cited source; the dated spot price stands in, under its own label, when
the exchange lists no such product), the price of your own trades that
day, the basis of other lots you priced that day, and today's unit
price coloured green or red against what you paid. Entering a basis
takes a price per unit or a total, whichever you type, and one entry on
a folded row covers every fill, split by quantity to the cent. The
editor opens as a full-width row under the lot.

**Smaller things.** File has New Window, which opens signed out, and
Refresh Assets. About credits ABK Labs, Inc., links to Report an Issue,
and opens from the menu when signed out; Help links open the browser.
A holding you entered by hand never reads as stale. A transfer booked
as income is described by the quantity that moved when the two legs
carry different money figures.

Everything else is 0.6.0.
