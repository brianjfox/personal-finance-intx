# Corbits Personal Finance 0.4.4

A brokerage that is mostly cash no longer trips a false *Position
Balance Mismatch*. Plaid reports cash as a holding, and the importer
was dropping it on the floor instead of counting it as the account's
cash balance, so reconciliation compared your options alone against
the whole account total. Cash now lands where it belongs, and the
investable-cash figure for Plaid brokerages is real for the first time.

Everything else is 0.4.3 — tax lots throughout, Coinbase and Kraken
lot derivation, and the menu-bar app.
