# Corbits Personal Finance 0.4.2

The app moves into your menu bar for good: net worth at a glance, a
one-click refresh, launch at login — and a host that keeps running for
the nightly imports.

## What changed

- **Your net worth in the menu bar.** Turn on *Show Net Worth* in the
  menu-bar menu and the icon carries the household figure ($15,390,927
  style, in your display currency), refreshed every minute and hidden
  whenever nobody is signed in. Your choice is remembered.
- **Refresh Assets.** One click in the menu-bar menu runs a full update
  across every institution and crypto address — the same pipeline the
  nightly runs.
- **Launch at Login.** A check item registers the app as a login item,
  so the host is alive for its nightly work from the moment you sign
  into your Mac.
- **The menu-bar icon owns the app's life.** Closing the window only
  hides it; the host — and the nightly imports — keep running. Only
  *Quit* (menu bar or ⌘Q) stops it. The Dock icon re-opens the window.
- **Launching feels alive.** The window opens instantly on a splash
  (the Corbits mark, "Starting the household host…") and switches to
  your dashboard the moment the host answers — no more blank window.
- The menu-bar icon is a proper monochrome template image, tinted by
  macOS for light and dark menu bars.

## From 0.4.0/0.4.1 (unpublished)

- **Tax lots throughout**: view every position's lots; enter a basis
  where it's unknown (prefilled with the value on the transfer date and
  the market unit price for that day); add lots by hand for
  institutions that report none; entries badged, journaled, durable.
- **Coinbase and Kraken tax lots** derived from their complete account
  records, reconciled exactly against balances.
- Long account ids ellipsize instead of stretching tables.

## After upgrading

Launch the app once and enable *Launch at Login* and *Show Net Worth*
from the menu-bar icon. Run an update on Coinbase and Kraken to
populate the lots.
