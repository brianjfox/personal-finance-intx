# Corbits Personal Finance 0.2.3

Reconnecting an institution no longer duplicates its accounts.

## What changed

- **Reconnecting keeps your accounts.** Relinking an existing Plaid or
  Enable Banking connection used to create a second copy of every
  account (and double-count them in net worth), because the provider
  issues brand-new account ids on each link. Accounts are now matched
  back to the ones the ledger already knows — by masked number and
  type, falling back to name and currency — so history, balances and
  transactions stay continuous across a relink, and movements the new
  link re-observes are recognized instead of booked twice.
- **Accounts that vanish from a feed are closed.** When a connection's
  complete account list stops reporting an open account, it is closed
  (and reopens by itself if the feed reports it again) instead of
  keeping its last balance in net worth forever.
- **`fin-host merge-accounts` repairs already-duplicated ledgers.** It
  detects duplicate pairs left behind by earlier reconnects (including
  the delete-and-re-add workaround), shows what it would merge, and with
  `--apply` folds each duplicate into the original account: history
  moves across, double-booked movements are voided, and nothing is
  deleted.
