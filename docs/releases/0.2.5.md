# Corbits Personal Finance 0.2.5

The app now cleans up after old reconnect damage by itself, keeps
institution cards honest, and lets you hide individual accounts.

## What changed

- **Duplicated accounts repair themselves.** A ledger that still holds
  duplicate account generations from reconnects made before 0.2.4 is
  detected and merged automatically when the app starts — history moves
  to the original account, double-booked movements are voided, and the
  repair is journaled. Only API-connected institutions are auto-merged;
  manually managed accounts are never touched.
- **Hide a single account.** Each connected account row has a hide
  button: the account leaves your totals immediately, updates stop
  recording it, and reconnecting the institution cannot bring it back.
  Hidden accounts are listed in the card's management area with a
  Restore button; restoring lets the next update fill it in again.
- **Closed accounts no longer appear on institution cards.** An account
  the feed stopped reporting (or a merged duplicate's leftover) used to
  render as a ghost row saying "not fetched yet"; it is now counted
  under "kept in history" instead.
- **"Did not answer tonight" clears itself.** A successful update now
  resolves the institution's stale connection warnings instead of
  leaving the banner up forever.
- **Developer-machine safety.** Running the project's test suite on a
  Mac could delete the app's real Keychain credentials; the sweep is now
  unreachable from tests. Error messages also point at the Assets page
  (the Institutions page no longer exists).
