# Corbits Personal Finance 0.2.6

Two quality-of-life features: reconnect from where the problem is
reported, and arrange your institutions your way.

## What changed

- **"missing — reconnect" is now a button.** When a connection token is
  missing, clicking those words on the Credentials card opens that
  institution's reconnect flow right there — Plaid launches in your
  browser, Enable Banking walks the consent flow, Coinbase and Kraken
  show their key forms — instead of sending you hunting for the
  institution's card.
- **Drag institutions into your own order.** On Assets, Cash &
  Holdings, hover a card to reveal a grip on its left edge and drag it
  where you want it. The order is saved and survives restarts; each tab
  (Institutions, Real Estate, Crypto) reorders independently, and the
  grip-only drag never interferes with the buttons and forms inside a
  card.
