# Corbits Personal Finance 0.5.0

Meet **the Ledger Analyst**, a third agent in the Strategy page's
dropdown beside the Strategist and the Estate Planner. Ask it what you
spent, what recurs, what a merchant cost you over a period, which month
was heaviest, or to show the lines behind a number. Your transactions
were always in the ledger — every nightly re-observes a rolling window
and books each movement as a dated fact — but the Strategist's scope is
deliberately aggregates only, so it could not read them. Now something
can, without widening what the Strategist sees.

The analyst reads and never writes: no journal, no findings, no
documents, no facts, no credentials, no orders. Every figure it quotes
is a field of a deterministic tool result, computed in code over cited
facts, and each number links back to the transaction it came from.
Totals are kept per currency and never mixed; only the cash-flow view
converts, at the ECB rate it names. Recurring charges are detected by
code, not by the model: a charge is listed when it repeats at a weekly,
biweekly, monthly, quarterly, or annual cadence with a consistent
amount, with its typical and latest amount, monthly equivalent, and the
date it is next expected.

Settings gains an engine choice for the Ledger Analyst on its own, so
transaction questions can stay on a local model while the Strategist
uses the cloud.

Two limits it states itself: history is only as deep as each account's
connection and backfill, and crypto connectors and manually entered
accounts carry no transactions yet.

Everything else is 0.4.4.
