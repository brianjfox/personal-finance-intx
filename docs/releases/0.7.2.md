# Corbits Personal Finance 0.7.2

**The app notices a night it did not start.** The header's net worth,
the Queue badge, and every open page used to refresh only when you did
something in the app. A nightly started by the scheduler, by Refresh
Assets in the tray, or from the command line changed the ledger with
nothing on screen hearing of it, so the top bar kept the figure from
when the app was opened. The app now watches the ledger every 30
seconds, and the moment the window comes back into view, and reloads
once per change. A People form with unsaved edits is left alone while
you type.

**The Queue badge and the Queue page never disagree.** The badge and
the Portfolio Health card could say four exceptions over a page saying
nothing needed your attention. They read one list now, and opening the
page refreshes it in place. A finding the nightly closes on its own is
announced the moment it closes, not only once the run finishes. A queue
that could not be loaded says so, with a retry, instead of reading as
"every account reconciled clean".

**Equal same-day fills on an exchange are not duplicates.** The first
night with Coinbase transactions raised 164 duplicate findings and held
the account: each was one advanced-trade order filled in equal chunks on
the same day, every fill under its own Coinbase id. Where an
institution's own ids identify its movements (Coinbase, Kraken, a
watch-only wallet), distinct ids are distinct movements, and the
detector leaves them be. The findings already raised clear themselves on
the next nightly.

**Hand-entered holdings stop coming back as stale.** A managed property
or account saved before 0.6 was re-read every night as if it had a feed,
and its Stale Balance finding returned after every dismissal. Every
figure in a managed account is one you typed; the app now knows that
whatever the saved file says, and the open findings clear on the next
nightly.

**The Strategist keeps your question while you look at the plan.**
Switching to Plan & Rebalancing with a question in flight dropped it,
and the answer, when it came, was never shown. The chat stays put across
the Strategy tabs now, and a slow reply stays on screen until the answer
is in the transcript.

Everything else is 0.7.1.
